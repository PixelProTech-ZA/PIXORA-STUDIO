HOW IT WORKS

Upload, instruct, compare.

  1. Upload a JPG, PNG or WEBP photograph.
  2. Describe the change in your own words.
  3. Choose what must stay the same. Each choice is added to the instruction sent to the AI model.
  4. Press TRANSFORM IMAGE and wait for the model.
  5. Compare with the slider and download the result.

PIXORA edits your photograph; it does not generate an unrelated picture. Results depend on the editing model the server is configured with, and preservation settings are requests to that model, not guarantees.

ACCOUNT

Privacy

PIXORA has no sign-in. Your creations live in this browser only and disappear if you clear site data.

When you press TRANSFORM IMAGE, your photograph and instruction are sent through the PIXORA server to Hugging Face for processing. The PIXORA server does not save or log image contents. Hugging Face and the model provider apply their own data policies.

The PIXORA server does not delete anything automatically because it stores nothing.
